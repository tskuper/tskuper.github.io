Events

  • Saturday 10:00 am - 8:00 pm: "Hell's Descent" - Descend into the bowels of hell with our haunted house. With high-grade special effects, from animatronics to hand-crafted props, and professional actors, this haunted house is highly rated for immersion and terror. You will believe the things you see, whether you want to or not.
  • Sunday 10:00am - 8:00 pm: "Slasher Sunday" - This is your chance to live out your fantasy of surviving in a horror movie. Our professional actors become terrifying hunters, both classic and new, and test your survival instincts. Can you survive this tournament-style fight for yor life? Winners are awarded special House of Nightmares merchandise as well as a trophy.

All weekend events are free for guests. There are no additional fees for attending as long as you have a room booked for the day of the event you are wanting to attend.

Halloween Special

We have something special planned this Halloween for all of our horror fanatics, for those who live for fear and truly want to be scared. While many of the haunts planned for our yearly Halloween event will be up to you to discover when the time comes, here are a few things you can expect:

  • Traditional Halloween Theme - Classic pumpkins, ghosts, witches, skeletons and the like will be the primary theme of this event.
  • Trick or Treat - We will have trick or treating with a terrifying twist. Beware, this is not your children's trick or treat.
  • Costume Contest - What Halloween event is complete without a good old fashioned costume contest? Winners of the event will be awarded amazing prizes, ranging from an all-expense-paid stay at our wonderful hotel to props and displays from years past. Reward specifics will be announced closer to time, so stay tuned!

There will be a $20 ticket fee for the Halloween special. This fee covers all aspects of the event, including food and drinks, and there will be no further charges for anything in the event.
