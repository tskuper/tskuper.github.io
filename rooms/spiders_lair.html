Spider's Lair

You've found yourself trapped in the spider's web. Instead of a bed, there is a hammock shaped like a spider web. The sound of thousands of legs skittering in the night plays softly. Web sacks hang from the ceiling, containing things you fear you don't want to know. You see things moving in the dark, too fast to make out. Can you survive the night when you swear that you can feel something crawling over your skin while you try to sleep?

  • Bathroom, complete with combined bathtub and shower, sink and toilet
