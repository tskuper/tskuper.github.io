Wails in the Night

A tail as old as time. Spirits from beyond the grave. Ghosts with an unsetlled debt. Specters of rage and jealousy of the living. In what is easily our most popular room, wails in the night will haunt your nightmares. Something tugs at your sheets. Something moves in the shadows, gliding just out of the edge of your vision. Furniture and pictures rattle and shake. Doors slam, things move when you aren't looking. Will you lose your nerve and let the spirits in?
