The Graveyard

Welcome to the graveyard. Your bed, a coffin. The headboard, your tombstone, changed for every guest. Hands claw through the floor. The windows are boarded, yet you see and hear the hoard through the cracks, trying to tear away your protection. You hear their unearthly groans throughout the night. Will you weather the relentless onslaught, or will you join their ranks?
