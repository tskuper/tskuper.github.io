Secrets of the Forest

They walk among us. They take our skin. They decieve us by pretending to be those we love or natural wildlife. Is the snake in the terrarium for looks? The staff don't remember putting it there, or ever feeding it for that matter. What about the birds that have been lurking outside the window? What about the knocking at your door, even though it's late and the staff don't come by at this time. You hear a voice that sounds familiar, yet inhuman at the same time coming from under the bed and from the wardrobe. Can you outsmart the trickster, or will you be another skin for it to wear?
