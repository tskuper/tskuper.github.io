Midnight at the Circus

Welcome to Midnight at the Circus. This room features a circus aesthetic. The furniture is reminiscant of what a carnie would have in their trailer. Built-in speakers play classic carnival music, however with an eerie undertone. The smell of popcorn permeates the room. Paintings of clowns adorn the room. Pay attention to them, because some guests have reported them moving or changing altogether. Others have even said that the clowns disappear from the painting as well. If they're not in the painting, they have to have gone somewhere...
